Email Setup

Connect EmailJS so contact form messages are delivered to your inbox — and visitors get an auto-reply.

Not configured
Setup Guide (one-time, 5 minutes)

Create a free EmailJS account

Go to emailjs.com and sign up for a free account. The free plan allows 200 emails/month — more than enough for a CV contact form.

Add an Email Service (connect your Gmail)

In the EmailJS dashboard go to Email Services → Add New Service. Choose Gmail, click Connect Account, and authorise with your Google account (raymondopiyo67@gmail.com). Copy the Service ID (looks like service_xxxxxxx).

Create Template 1 — "Message to Raymond" (notification to you)

Go to Email Templates → Create New Template. Use the content below, then save and copy the Template ID (template_xxxxxxx).

Subject: New CV Message: {{subject}} To: raymondopiyo67@gmail.com You have a new message from your CV contact form: Name: {{from_name}} Email: {{reply_to}} Subject: {{subject}} Message: {{message}} --- Reply directly to this email to respond to {{from_name}}.

In the template settings set To Email to raymondopiyo67@gmail.com and Reply To to {{reply_to}}.

Create Template 2 — "Auto-Reply to Visitor" (confirmation to sender)

Create a second template. Set To Email to {{to_email}}. This is the automatic reply the visitor receives after submitting the form.

Subject: Thanks for reaching out, {{to_name}}! Hi {{to_name}}, Thank you for contacting me through my CV. I have received your message regarding "{{subject}}" and will get back to you as soon as possible. Your message: {{message}} Best regards, Raymond Okinyi Diploma in Computer Science | NYS Engineering Institute, Nairobi 📧 raymondopiyo67@gmail.com | 📞 0795058238

Copy the Template ID for this one too.

Get your Public Key

Go to Account → General in the EmailJS dashboard. Copy your Public Key (looks like aBcDeFgHiJkLmNoPq).

Paste your keys below and save

Fill in the form below with the three values you copied and click Save Keys. The contact form on your CV will immediately start sending real emails.

How it works after setup
Visitor fills form

Name, email, subject and message on your CV site.

EmailJS delivers it

The message arrives in your Gmail inbox with the sender's email as reply-to.

Visitor gets auto-reply

They receive a confirmation email from your Gmail immediately.

You reply via Gmail

Hit Reply in Gmail — your response goes straight to the visitor's inbox.
